Forgotten things don't vanish. They wait.
The glove under the bed. The unread book. The paint that dried in its tray.

These are the Hamartia: the spirits of things that were loved, then left unplayed. They can't be banished. They can only be played with, or passed on to someone who will.

1Sweep the room slowly. They settle into corners, low and high. The amber arrow and the hum guide you.
2Hold the reticle on one until it notices you noticing it.
3Then do the real thing: play, or pass it on.
